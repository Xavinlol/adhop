# Privacy

Adhop collects no data. It makes no network requests, has no analytics and no tracking.

It runs only on youtube.com. It reads the page to find ads, the Skip button and the channel of the video you watch, so your rules can apply. None of this leaves your browser.

Your settings are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, so your browser can sync them between your own devices if you have sync turned on. The ad counter and time saved are saved with `chrome.storage.local` and stay on your device. Removing Adhop deletes both.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/Xavinlol/adhop/issues).
